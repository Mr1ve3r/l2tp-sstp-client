First release of this fork.

Adds SSTP over TLS on port 443 alongside the inherited L2TP/IPsec: PAP, CHAP,
MSCHAPv2 and EAP-MSCHAPv2, and support for reaching the server through an HTTP
proxy.

Adds a server certificate store with system, custom-CA-only and pinned
policies, a pre-flight check that says what a profile will accept before it
connects, and failover groups that try profiles in order and stop on an
authentication failure rather than spreading failed logins across servers.

Profiles and their secrets now live on the Android side, with secrets held
under an Android Keystore key.

Not yet verified on a device across the full protocol and Android-version
matrix; proxy-only mode, inherited from upstream, does not work.
